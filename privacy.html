Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect a TikTok account, the App receives from TikTok: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and OAuth access and refresh tokens. When uploading, the App reads the creator information TikTok returns (such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length).

2. How we use it

This data is used only to upload videos you have chosen to your own TikTok account and to show which account is connected. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the App's private server in a file readable only by the App, and are never shared, sold, or transferred to third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok's official API over HTTPS.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account. Disconnecting in the App deletes the stored tokens immediately. You may also revoke access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or email us to request deletion.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
